Download prompt

Help me build my first small web app. Ask me what problem I want to solve, who will use it, and what one task they need to complete. Then build a mobile-friendly prototype with up to three features and sample data. Provide complete source files and preview instructions. Show me how to test the main task, fix issues one at a time, and publish a link. Explain whether data stays in the browser or syncs between devices.

Build, test and share

  1. Paste the prompt into your AI tool and answer its questions.
  2. Open the preview, complete the main task and reload to check what was saved.
  3. Describe any bug with the steps, expected result and actual result. Test the fix on your phone.
  4. Publish your app and open its link in a private window before sharing.
