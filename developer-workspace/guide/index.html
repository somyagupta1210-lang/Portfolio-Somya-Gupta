A few things to know.

Your data stays in your browser. It does not sync across devices or get shared with other visitors. Export a backup before clearing site data or changing device. Keep passwords and API keys out of your snippets.
Do I need to pay for AI or hosting?

The starter itself is free and needs no AI API. You can ask an AI tool to help customize it using whatever access you have. Each AI and hosting service has its own availability and usage limits.

Can I add this to my portfolio?

Yes. Customize it, understand how it works, and describe your own changes honestly. Keep the original license and creator attribution. Sample tasks are examples, not proof of completed client work.

Where do I change my name and photo?

In config.js, edit the name, subtitle, photo path and accent. Replace the included Ameer Hamza portrait with your own photo. The AI can do this for you.

Why doesn't double-clicking index.html work?

This app uses JavaScript modules, so open it through a local HTTP server or a hosted URL. Ask your AI for the simplest method on your computer.

Can I use this for team work?

This version is a personal browser-local workspace. Shared accounts, team access, reminders and cloud sync are not included.
