Will this work if I am not a developer?

Yes. The first questions establish your field and goal. The prompt adapts the skills, work samples, wording and visual direction to your profession.

What if I have no professional experience yet?

Use genuine coursework, personal projects, volunteer work or other relevant responsibilities. The prompt must not invent jobs, certificates or client results.

Will the AI upload everything for me?

Only if it has suitable account-editing tools and your authorization. Otherwise it should give you ready-to-paste text, image files and a simple upload checklist. A preview is not proof of an upload.

What if my AI cannot generate images or PDFs?

Ask it for the precise design prompts and editable text. Use an image or design tool to create the assets. It should explain its limits, not pretend it made files.

Can my LinkedIn page have interactive 3D or animation?

The 3D appearance comes from artwork in banners, covers and documents. Native LinkedIn sections keep their own layout. This kit does not add custom website code to your profile.

What should I check before publishing?

Check your name, dates, claims, photo crop, mobile readability, links and public contact choices. Label demos accurately. Do not share passwords or confidential client information.
