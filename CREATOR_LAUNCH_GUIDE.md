## ManyChat flow

Verified setup reference, checked 30 October 2026:
https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/16654065283100-Quick-Automation-Auto-DM-links-from-comments

Connect the intended Instagram account. In ManyChat choose Automations > New Automation > Auto-DM links from comments. Select the specific Reel, or Next post or reel when preparing before publication. Set the keyword to portfolio. Enable an optional public reply and Opening DM. Use an opening button to invite the person to continue; then send the repository and guide link. Skip email collection and follow gating for a short delivery flow. Preview, activate, then test the full sequence from another account. Check Sends and Clicks in Insights. Features depend on the connected account and available plan.

The Opening DM button opens the conversation for further messages within the documented 24-hour window. A reminder, if enabled, must follow the platform's allowed messaging window. No automation is activated by this kit.
